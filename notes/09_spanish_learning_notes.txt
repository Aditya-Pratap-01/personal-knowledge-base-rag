Spanish Learning Notes - Week 6

Verb conjugation practice - present tense regular verbs:
- hablar (to speak): hablo, hablas, habla, hablamos, hablais, hablan
- comer (to eat): como, comes, come, comemos, comeis, comen
- vivir (to live): vivo, vives, vive, vivimos, vivis, viven

Common phrases learned this week:
- "Que tal?" - How's it going / what's up
- "Mas o menos" - So-so / more or less
- "No pasa nada" - No worries / it's fine

Grammar note: ser vs estar - ser for permanent characteristics (soy alto - I am tall),
estar for temporary states or location (estoy cansado - I am tired, estoy en casa -
I am at home).

Goal for next week: learn past tense (preterite) conjugations for regular -ar, -er,
-ir verbs. Also practice listening with a short podcast episode daily.
