Weekly Workout Split

Monday - Push (Chest, Shoulders, Triceps)
- Bench press 4x8
- Incline dumbbell press 3x10
- Shoulder press 3x10
- Lateral raises 3x15
- Tricep pushdowns 3x12

Wednesday - Pull (Back, Biceps)
- Deadlift 4x6
- Lat pulldown 3x10
- Barbell rows 3x10
- Face pulls 3x15
- Bicep curls 3x12

Friday - Legs
- Squats 4x8
- Leg press 3x10
- Romanian deadlift 3x10
- Leg curls 3x12
- Calf raises 4x15

Saturday - optional cardio or mobility day, 30-40 min incline walk.

Note: increase weight only when all reps are hit with good form for two
consecutive sessions. Track lifts in a separate log.
