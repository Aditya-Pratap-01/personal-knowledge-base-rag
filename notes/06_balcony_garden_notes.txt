Balcony Garden Notes

Current plants: tulsi, mint, curry leaf plant, money plant, marigold, a small
chili plant.

Watering schedule: tulsi and mint need daily watering in summer, every alternate
day in winter. Curry leaf plant is more drought tolerant - water every 2-3 days.
Marigold needs full sun, at least 5-6 hours daily to flower well.

Fertilizing: use diluted vermicompost tea once every 2 weeks in growing season.
Avoid over-fertilizing chili plants - too much nitrogen gives leafy growth but
fewer chilies.

Pest watch: check curry leaf plant for aphids/mealybugs regularly, they love it
especially in humid weather. Neem oil spray (diluted) once every 10 days as
prevention.

Repotting note: money plant needs repotting when roots start showing through
drainage holes, roughly once a year, into a pot one size bigger.
