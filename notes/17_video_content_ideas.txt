Video Content Ideas List

1. "Reacting to old gameplay from 5 years ago" - nostalgia format, easy to edit
2. Collaboration video idea with another creator in the same gaming niche
3. Behind-the-scenes / setup tour video - viewers often ask about gear used
4. Tier list video ranking games in a specific genre
5. "Trying a game genre I never play" - fresh reaction content, good for
   engagement since it's unpredictable
6. Q&A video from community-submitted questions, do this once a quarter
7. Speedrun attempt video, even if casual/non-competitive, these tend to
   perform well with a countdown timer overlay

Editing note: videos with a strong hook in the first 10 seconds retain
viewers noticeably better based on past analytics - lead with the most
exciting moment, not a slow intro.

Thumbnail note: high contrast colors and an expressive face thumbnail
consistently outperform plain gameplay screenshots in click-through rate.
