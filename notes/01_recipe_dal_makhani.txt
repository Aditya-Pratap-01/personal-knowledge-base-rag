Dal Makhani Recipe

Ingredients: 1 cup whole black urad dal, 1/4 cup rajma (kidney beans), soaked overnight.
2 tbsp butter, 1 tbsp ghee, 1 onion finely chopped, 2 tomatoes pureed,
1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 1 tsp red chili powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric,
1/2 cup fresh cream, salt to taste.

Method:
1. Pressure cook the soaked dal and rajma with water and salt for 6-7 whistles until soft.
2. In a pan, heat butter and ghee. Add onions and saute until golden.
3. Add ginger-garlic paste, cook for 2 minutes.
4. Add tomato puree and spices, cook until oil separates.
5. Add the cooked dal along with its water. Simmer on low heat for at least 45 minutes,
   stirring occasionally so it doesn't stick.
6. Mash a little of the dal against the pan to thicken the gravy.
7. Finish with cream and a knob of butter. Garnish with coriander.

Tip: The longer it simmers on low flame, the better it tastes. Restaurants often
let it simmer for hours on a tawa/dum setup.
