Manali Trip Plan - 5 Days

Day 1: Arrive Manali by overnight Volvo from Delhi. Check into hotel near Old Manali.
Rest, evening walk along the river, dinner at Old Manali cafes.

Day 2: Local sightseeing - Hadimba Temple, Manu Temple, Club House, Vashisht hot water
springs. Try trout fish at a riverside cafe in the evening.

Day 3: Solang Valley - paragliding and zorbing (weather permitting). Cable car ride
for valley views. Return by evening.

Day 4: Day trip to Rohtang Pass / Atal Tunnel depending on road conditions and permits.
Need to arrange permit a day in advance from the SDM office or via a local travel agent.
Carry warm clothes, snow boots can be rented near the pass.

Day 5: Free morning for shopping at Mall Road (shawls, wooden crafts, dry fruits).
Departure by evening Volvo back to Delhi.

Budget estimate: hotel ~1800/night, Volvo tickets ~1200 one way, activities ~3000
per person for Solang Valley.

Packing note: layers are essential, temperature drops sharply at night even in
non-winter months.
