Tech Troubleshooting Log

Issue: Wi-Fi router randomly disconnecting every few hours.
Fix attempted: changed wireless channel from auto to a fixed less-congested
channel (channel 6 for 2.4GHz), reduced dropouts significantly.
Also updated router firmware to the latest version, which resolved a known
bug mentioned in release notes about DHCP lease renewal causing drops.

Issue: External hard drive not detected on one USB port.
Fix: turned out to be a power delivery issue on a USB 3.0 port when drive
was daisy-chained through a hub. Connecting directly to a rear USB port
on the PC (higher power output) fixed it consistently.

Issue: Webcam lag during video calls with poor lighting.
Fix: added a small ring light setup, dramatically improved video quality
without needing to change camera hardware since better lighting reduces
the sensor's need to compensate digitally.

Issue: Printer showing offline despite being powered on and connected.
Fix: printer spooler service needed a restart on the PC; also cleared
old stuck print jobs from the queue which was blocking new ones.
