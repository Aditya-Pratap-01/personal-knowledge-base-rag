Gift Ideas List

For Dad's birthday: a good quality leather wallet, or a woolen shawl since he's
mentioned the old one is worn out. He also likes reading, so a biography of a
freedom fighter could work.

For Mom: she mentioned wanting a pressure cooker with a bigger capacity for
family gatherings. Also consider a nice saree since she liked one at a recent
wedding.

For friend's housewarming: a scented candle set, or a small indoor plant with
a decorative pot. Practical option would be a good quality cutting board set.

For colleague leaving the company: a personalized notebook/journal, or a
coffee mug with a funny work-related quote, kept it light since we're not
extremely close.

General note: always check if the person has any allergies or preferences
before gifting food items or scented products.
