Home Maintenance Checklist

Monthly:
- Check RO water filter cartridge indicator
- Clean AC filters (especially before summer season starts)
- Test smoke detectors / check fire extinguisher pressure gauge

Quarterly:
- Inverter battery water top-up (distilled water only)
- Deep clean chimney/exhaust filters in kitchen
- Check for termite activity near wooden furniture and window frames

Yearly:
- Full AC servicing (gas top-up check) before summer
- Waterproofing check on terrace before monsoon
- Pest control treatment for whole house
