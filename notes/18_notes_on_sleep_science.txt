Notes on Sleep Science (from articles read)

Sleep cycles last roughly 90 minutes each, cycling through light sleep, deep
sleep, and REM sleep. Deep sleep is when most physical recovery happens,
REM sleep is linked to memory consolidation and dreaming.

Consistency matters more than people think - going to bed and waking up at
the same time daily (even on weekends) helps regulate the circadian rhythm
better than just getting a fixed number of hours inconsistently.

Blue light from screens close to bedtime can delay melatonin release, pushing
the body's natural sleep signal later. Using night mode or avoiding screens
30-60 minutes before bed is commonly recommended.

Caffeine has a half-life of roughly 5-6 hours, so a coffee at 4 PM can still
affect sleep onset at 10 PM for many people.

Practical takeaway noted: aim for a consistent wind-down routine (dim lights,
no screens, maybe reading) starting about 45 minutes before intended sleep time.
