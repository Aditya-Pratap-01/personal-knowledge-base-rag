Monthly Budget Categories (Template)

Fixed expenses: rent/EMI, utility bills, internet, phone recharge, insurance
premiums (these stay roughly the same every month).

Variable essentials: groceries, fuel/transport, household supplies (these
fluctuate but are still necessary spends).

Lifestyle/discretionary: eating out, subscriptions (streaming, gym), shopping,
entertainment (movies, outings).

Savings/investments: SIP contributions, emergency fund top-up, any recurring
deposit.

Rule of thumb followed: try to keep discretionary spending under a fixed
percentage of take-home income, and automate savings/investment transfers
right after salary credit so it happens before discretionary spending eats
into it.

Review cadence: do a quick review of spends across these categories at the
end of every month to catch any category that's creeping up over time.
