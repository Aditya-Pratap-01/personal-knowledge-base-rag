Pet Care Notes - Dog (Labrador, 2 years old)

Feeding schedule: two meals a day, 7 AM and 7 PM. Currently on a dry kibble brand,
roughly 2 cups per meal, adjusted based on activity level and vet's guidance during
checkups.

Vaccination schedule: annual rabies booster due every year around the same month.
DHPPi booster also on annual cycle. Keep the vaccination card updated and carry
it to every vet visit.

Grooming: brush coat twice a week to manage shedding, more often during shedding
season. Nail trimming roughly once a month or when clicking is heard on the floor.
Ear cleaning once every 2 weeks with a vet-recommended solution.

Exercise: minimum 30-40 minutes of walk/play daily, Labradors are energetic and
prone to weight gain if under-exercised. Also enjoys fetch in the evening.

Vet notes: mild seasonal skin allergy noticed in humid months last year - vet
recommended an anti-allergy shampoo, used once every 10 days during that season.
